# LinkWise AI Features 🌟

Detailed breakdown of the capabilities integrated into LinkWise AI.

## 1. Intelligence Extraction Engine
- **Extraction Depth Control**: Choose between **Quick Scan** (fast, surface-level analysis) and **Deep Dive** (comprehensive multi-page intelligence) protocols.
- **URL Analysis**: Scans target websites for core business information, value propositions, and key metrics.
- **Contextual Understanding**: LLM-powered parsing that understands the *meaning* of content, not just the keywords.
- **Company Profiles**: Automatically identifies HQ locations, employee counts, and social footprints.

## 2. Interactive AI Assistant
- **Context-Aware Chat**: The built-in chatbot knows what you just extracted and can answer specific questions about it.
- **Market Analysis**: Ask the AI to compare the extracted data against market trends.
- **Strategy Suggestions**: Get AI-generated advice based on the intelligence gathered.

## 3. Premium Design System
- **Glassmorphism Architecture**: Modern UI using backdrop blurs and subtle white-space borders.
- **Advanced Bento Grids**: High-density data visualization for complex extraction results.
- **Custom Typography**: Intentional pairing of **Outfit** for bold headings and **Inter** for high-legibility body copy.
- **Responsive Fluidity**: Desktop-first precision with mobile-first logic, optimized for all screen sizes.
- **Micro-Animations**: Smooth scale transitions, staggered entrances, and hover-state logic for higher engagement.

## 4. Robust Infrastructure
- **Intelligent Error Recovery**: Graceful handling of API rate limits (429) and permission issues with retry suggestions.
- **Zero-Trust Logic**: Secure handling of API keys and server-side interactions.
- **Modular Architecture**: Clean TypeScript codebase leveraging modern ESM and production-grade patterns.

## 5. Strategic Intelligence Workflow
- **Gap Analysis**: Identifies critically missing elements and growth opportunities (High-conviction insights).
- **Market Rivalry Mapping**: Deep analysis of competitors and reasons for market competition.
- **Multi-Channel Strategic Outreach**: Generates tailored messages for **Email**, **LinkedIn**, and **X/Twitter** based on specific business gaps.
- **Outreach Customization**: Fine-tune drafts by specifying **Target Contact Roles**, **Custom Call-to-Actions (CTAs)**, and focusing on specific **Pain Points**.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Automatic detection of brand sentiment (Positive/Neutral/Negative).
- **Executive Synopses**: High-level summaries generated for quick stakeholder review.
- **History Tracking**: Keeps track of recent extractions for quick reference.
